DIGITAL X SUSTAIN is a two-day, hands-on event on the sustainability of our
digital and physical worlds.

While the digital transition is seen as a motor to enable green
transitions, this perspective also has some critics. Researchers and civil
society organizations have been drawing attention to potential
discrepancies between digital and sustainability goals. They are notably
highlighting questions around public participation, social impacts, and the
environmental impact of digitalization, including electricity consumption,
water consumption, extraction of rare materials, and e-waste, all of which
require public discourse and policy reforms.

While adoption and criticism occur in parallel, proponents and opponents
rarely meet in dialogue. This limits creative innovations that can be born
out of constructive disagreements. We aim to bring various perspectives
into focus by inviting stakeholders from different sectors to engage in
dialogue.

We invite practitioners from public, private, and associative sectors, as
well as researchers, artists, designers, and residents, who engage in
projects that foster or question the sustainability issues related to the
internet and digital technologies, to present their work and ideas in
traditional and innovative formats.
