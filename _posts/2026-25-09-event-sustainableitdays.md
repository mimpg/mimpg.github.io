On 14 and 15 October 2026, MImPG (Metropolitan Internet Meets Post-Growth) took part in the Brussels Sustainable IT Days at the Maison de la Poste, Tour & Taxis.
The event was the first edition of the Brussels Sustainable IT Days, organized by the Belgian Institute for Sustainable IT with Bruxelles Environnement and held during Brussels Climate Week. The event brought public, private, and academic actors together around the sustainability questions about the digital sector, so it was a good place to put our work in front of people who work on them every day. 

MImPG is a transdisciplinary research project that investigates the opportunities for post-growth metropolitan internet access as a way to reduce the environmental impact of digital technologies. The team brings together researchers from ULB and UCLouvain and works on the Brussels region. Much of the debate on sustainable IT focuses on making equipment and data centers more efficient. We ask a broader question: what internet does a city need, and who decides how much of it we build and use? 

We presented the project in the Exhibition Village through a demo of the arbitration idea. Visitors could try and see the impacts of a prioritisation system on their uses such as viseo, video conference or web browsing. They were able to discuss the trade-offs it raises with us. Those conversations will feed directly into our next steps.

Our work continues. We are organizing a two-day event at usquare on 17 and 18 March 2027. Please join us if you want to learn more about the sustainability of digital worlds, get inspired, and imagine a more frugal internet for Brussels.
